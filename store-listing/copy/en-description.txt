GUGA Knowledge is your browser’s personal knowledge inbox. Save useful pages, highlights, images and passing ideas, then bring them together with tags and projects so you can return to what you collect.

Capture now, organize offline
Save links and selected text from the context menu, a keyboard shortcut or the sidebar. Write Markdown notes, paste images, record audio when you choose, or capture the visible part of a page. Records stay in your current browser, ready to revisit and organize offline.

Give your notes a place in your projects
Add multiple tags and link a record to more than one project. Use descriptions, subprojects and relationship graphs to connect material to your goals. Filter organized records by project membership to find material you have not put to use yet.

Keep your reading queue together
Collect pages into web groups and mark them as read. Move individual pages between groups, or drag a whole group into a project, onto a tag or into another group.

Your knowledge in every new tab
When enabled, GUGA opens as your Chrome new-tab page. Edit Todo and Idea lists, revisit recent favorites, continue unread web groups and open project cards. Add everyday sites to the Dock and choose how many favorite cards appear in settings.

Your library, on your terms
Connect your own Google Drive account and decide when to download or upload. Records, projects, relationships, tag definitions and Dock sites sync; images and audio are stored separately. Export a full ZIP organized by project or tag, with readable original Markdown names and attachments for browsing and backup.

Make it comfortable
Choose English, Chinese, French or Japanese, six color themes, fonts and a page companion. Sort records in several ways. Editing keeps your card in place until you reopen the view or re-sort.

Offline use needs no GUGA account. Google Drive is optional, and sync starts only when you request it. No ads, usage analytics or automatic AI processing. Export a backup or confirm your cloud data is complete before uninstalling.
